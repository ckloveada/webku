Deep Dive: Language Features of Descriptive Text

To write or analyze descriptive text effectively, students must master its 5 Core Language Features. Each element makes descriptions vivid, accurate, and professional. (Untuk menulis atau menganalisis teks deskriptif, siswa harus menguasai 5 Unsur Kebahasaan Utama ini agar deskripsi menjadi hidup, akurat, dan profesional.)

Feature #1

Specific Object (Fokus pada Objek Spesifik)

Unlike Report Texts that describe a general category of things (e.g., Computers, Offices, Trees), a Descriptive Text focuses on only one specific entity that possesses unique characteristics.

General Object (Report Text)

"Offices use furniture."

Refers to all offices in the entire world without specific context.

Specific Object (Descriptive Text)

"The Front Desk of SMK Negeri 1 Palangka Raya."

Refers to one designated reception desk in a specific local institution.

Office & Local Context Examples

General Object (Report) Specific Object (Descriptive Text) Why It Is Specific
Paper Shredder The Heavy-Duty Shredder in Room 204 Describes a specific machine located in a designated school lab.
Secretaries Mrs. Anita, the Executive Secretary of Governor's Office Focuses on one individual professional figure.
Traditional Houses Huma Betang of Desa Sangai Describes one unique traditional longhouse in Central Kalimantan.

Feature #2

Simple Present Tense (Penggunaan Tata Bahasa Masa Kini)

Descriptive text uses the Simple Present Tense because it expresses timeless facts, general truths, current conditions, and daily habitual actions.

A. Verbal Sentences (Action / State Verbs)
Subject + Verb 1 (-s/-es) + Object/Complement
Singular Subject (He, She, It, The Machine, The Receptionist) $\rightarrow$ Add -s / -es to the verb.

Example: The receptionist greets guests politely every morning.

Plural Subject (They, We, You, I, The Files, The Clerks) $\rightarrow$ Base Verb (No -s/-es).

Example: Administrative assistants organize incoming letters carefully.

B. Nominal Sentences (Linking Verbs / Be)
Subject + am / is / are + Adjective / Noun / Adverb
Singular Subject + is

The Kahayan Conference Hall is spacious and well-ventilated.

Plural Subject + are

The official archives are stored in moisture-resistant boxes.

Feature #4

Action Verbs (Kata Kerja Aksi)

Action verbs describe physical actions or movements performed by a subject. In office administration, action verbs map directly onto administrative workflow stages.

Feature #5

Sensory Words / Imagery (Kata-Kata Panca Indera)

Sensory words appeal to human senses (Sight, Sound, Touch, Smell, Taste) to build a vivid mental picture of the environment being described.

SIGHT (Penglihatan)

  • • Brightly lit
  • • Cluttered / Organized
  • • Glossy finish
  • • Neon-labeled

SOUND (Pengendengan)

  • • Quiet workspace
  • • Humming printer
  • • Clicking keyboards
  • • Soft acoustic chime

TOUCH (Perabaan)

  • • Smooth wooden surface
  • • Cold metallic handles
  • • Soft cushioned seat
  • • Textured paper

SMELL (Penciuman)

  • • Fresh paper aroma
  • • Aromatic coffee scent
  • • Sanitized air
  • • Polished mahogany smell

Guided Text Analysis Module

Extracting 5 Language Features from a sample administrative text

Miss Dayang is a senior administrative secretary at the Governor's Office in Palangka Raya. She works in a brightly lit and neat workspace. Every morning, she warmly receives official guests and prints important daily schedules using a high-speed laser printer. Her desk features a smooth mahogany finish decorated with a small wooden statue inspired by Dayak carving.

Comprehensive Feature Extraction Table

Language Feature Extracted Example from Text Explanation / Function
1. Specific Object Miss Dayang (Senior Secretary) Describes one specific individual, not secretaries in general.
2. Simple Present Tense she works / she receives / prints Uses Present Tense (S + V1-s) to state daily work habits.
3. Adjectives senior, neat, high-speed, small, wooden Modifies nouns to give detailed qualities of the office setting.
4. Action Verbs receives, prints, works Shows physical workplace activities performed by the secretary.
5. Sensory Words brightly lit (Sight), smooth mahogany (Touch) Helps the reader visualize lighting and feel the desk texture.
